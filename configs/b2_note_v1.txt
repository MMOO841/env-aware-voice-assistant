About the situation text: it comes from automatic tools, not from a person. Speech recognition writes the words, speaker identification decides whether a voice is the user's, and a sound detector lists the sounds it detected, each with a confidence between 0 and 1. All three can be wrong. The sound detector sometimes reports a sound that did not happen, for example because of background noise or voices, and sometimes misses one that did; detections with lower confidence are wrong more often. Treat each detected sound as evidence, weighed by its confidence and by what the transcript says, not as a fact.
