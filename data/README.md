# Data

This folder holds only a few example scenes. The full dataset (36 GB of rendered audio) is not stored
in the repository: it is generated by the scripts in `src/` from public recordings and openly
licensed models.

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`samples/`](samples/) | Six example scenes from the training split, covering all five test parts: the 8 s window at every decision point (16 kHz FLAC), the full audio of the two multi-turn episodes, and one JSON file per scene with the words, the sounds, the gold stop / respond answers and the stored front-end outputs (transcripts, speaker tags, detected sounds, situation text). 5.3 MB. Format: [`samples/README.md`](samples/README.md); credits and licence (CC BY-SA 3.0): [`samples/CREDITS.md`](samples/CREDITS.md). |

**Development, test and replication audio is not released.** The only audio in this repository is
the six training scenes in `samples/`.

## The dataset

Synthetic household scenes at 16 kHz: text-to-speech dialogue (Kokoro), real recorded event and
household sounds (FSD50K and Freesound), simulated room reverberation (OpenSLR 28) and recorded
background noise (DEMAND), with real TV speech (VoxPopuli) in some scenes. A scene is a group of
versions that differ only in what the contrast is about (minimal pairs): the sound, with identical
words, or the user's reaction to the same sound. Multi-turn episodes have one version. Every split
also has single-version control scenes for the basic cases: a question to the assistant during which
a ring or knock starts, a remark to the assistant about a ring or knock, an interruption while the
assistant talks, and silence while it talks.

Training, development and test share no source: recordings (grouped by uploader and by "same sound"
audio fingerprints), text-to-speech voices, rooms, noise environments, TV speakers and conversations
each belong to one of them. The replication split is a second test split with its own recordings, TV
speakers and conversations, and the test split's voices, rooms and noise environments. Dialogue lines
belong to one split, except a few short common lines (such as "Hello?" and backchannels such as
"Uh-huh.") that occur in every split. The training split was generated in 12 random draws of the
single-line parts (each draw a new set of scenes built from the same training sources) and 4 renders
of each episode script.

Size of the full renders (one 16-bit WAV per decision point, one FLAC per episode, and a `meta.jsonl`
per folder with every source, time and gold label):

| Split | Render folders | 8 s windows (decision points) | Full episode mixes | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training | 12 (`train_d00` to `train_d11`) | 85,954 | 3,510 | 25.0 GB |
| Development | 1 | 3,692 | 194 | 1.1 GB |
| Test | 1 | 16,302 | 775 | 4.9 GB |
| Replication | 1 | 16,248 | 777 | 4.9 GB |
| **Total** | 15 | 122,196 | 5,256 | 35.9 GB |

The training renders hold 39,510 scenes: 3,600 groups for each of the four single-line parts, 3,510
episode renders and 21,600 control scenes. Noise-degraded copies of the development, test and
replication scenes used in the robustness analyses are not counted above.

These must be downloaded to regenerate the dataset. The paths are where the scripts expect them,
relative to the repository root.

| Source | Used for | Licence | Expected location |
|---|---|---|---|
| FSD50K (Fonseca et al., 2022), https://zenodo.org/records/4060432: the doc, ground-truth and metadata archives, and the clips selected from the dev and eval audio archives | Event sounds (phone, doorbell, knock), unrelated household sounds, footsteps, door and cupboard or drawer sounds | Dataset CC BY 4.0; only clips under CC0 or CC BY 3.0 are used | `data/fsd50k_dl/` (archives), `data/fsd50k_dl/x/` (extracted metadata), `data/fsd50k_sel/<clip id>.wav` (selected clips) |
| Freesound API, https://freesound.org (needs API credentials) | Recordings uploaded on or after 2023-06-01 (doorbells, knocks, rings, vibrations, intercoms, doors), test and replication splits only | CC0 or CC BY | `data/freesound_new/raw/`, `data/freesound_doors/raw/` |
| DEMAND (Thiemann et al., 2013), https://zenodo.org/records/1227121: the 17 `<ENV>_16k.zip` files | Background noise, split by environment (channel 1 of the 16 kHz recordings) | CC BY-SA 3.0 | `data/demand_dl/`, extracted to `data/demand_x/<ENV>/ch01.wav` |
| OpenSLR 28, RIRS_NOISES (Ko et al., 2017), https://www.openslr.org/28/ | Simulated room impulse responses, split by room | Apache-2.0 | `data/rirs_dl/rirs_noises.zip`, extracted to `data/rirs_x/RIRS_NOISES/` |
| VoxPopuli (Wang et al., 2021), Hugging Face `facebook/voxpopuli`, English test file `en_test-00000-of-00001.parquet`, revision 42f0187 | Real TV and radio speech, split by speaker | CC0 | `data/voxpopuli_dl/`, utterances written to `data/tv_speech/<split>/` |
| Wikimedia Commons: `Telephone.ogg`, `Model_500_Telephone_British_ring.ogg`, `Doorbell-classic-dingdong.ogg`, `Knock_on_door.wav` | Four extra event recordings, test split only | See each file's Commons page | `data/commons4_testonly/` |
| MUSAN (Snyder et al., 2015), https://www.openslr.org/17/, noise part | Only to find and remove our clips that also occur in MUSAN; nothing from MUSAN is mixed into a scene | Not redistributed | `data/musan_dl/musan/noise/` |

Models used to render: Kokoro-82M v1.0 (`hexgrad/Kokoro-82M`, revision f3ff357, Apache-2.0), loaded
from the Hugging Face cache, for the users, the visitors and the synthetic TV voices; Piper
`en_US-libritts-high`
(`rhasspy/piper-voices`, revision 6b9cfaa) in `data/models_dl/piper_libritts_high_6b9cfaa/`, which
gives the assistant's speaking times in every split (the assistant's voice itself is mixed in only in
a small echo subset of the test and replication splits, at a low level) and the user voices of the
second-engine subsets of those two splits. The dialogue lines and episode scripts were
generated with a language model through its API (a key file is passed on the command line); a new run
gives different lines.

## Generating the dataset

Run from the repository root (`python -X utf8 src/<script> ...`; the Kokoro environment of
`requirements-data.txt`, CPU only for steps 1 to 3). In order:

1. **Recording, voice, room and noise splits (step 0).** These scripts select the clips and fix the
   splits. Their outputs are not shipped, because they list the held-out test and replication material.
   - `src/step0_fsd50k_counts.py` and `src/step0_curate_pools.py`: count and curate the FSD50K event and
     decoy pools from the metadata; `src/fsd50k_extract.py`: extract the selected clips from the FSD50K
     archives.
   - `src/step0_fingerprint.py`: group recordings of the same sound by audio fingerprint;
     `src/step0_musan_match.py`: remove clips that also occur in MUSAN.
   - `src/step0_freesound_subset.py` (with `src/freesound_client.py`; needs a Freesound API key):
     recordings uploaded after June 2023.
   - `src/step0_allocate.py`: assign the recording groups to test, replication, development and training;
     `src/step0_split_aux.py`: seeded splits of voices, rooms and background noise.
   - `src/step0_generate_lines.py` and `src/step0_assign_lines.py`: generate the first set of
     conversations and lines with a language model (API key) and split them after the line check.
   - `src/step0v4_new_classes.py` and `src/step0v4_freesound_doors.py`: the footstep, door and
     cupboard/drawer classes and the later-uploaded door sounds; `src/step0v4_tv_speech.py`: TV and radio
     voices from VoxPopuli; `src/step0v4_speaker_overlap.py`: check that the speaker-ID model's training
     data does not overlap our voices; `src/step0v4_rir_positions.py`: source positions in each
     simulated room.

   `src/p1v4_units.py` reads their outputs: `results/step0/{aux_splits, line_assignment,
   split_final, event_decoy_pools, freesound_new_subset}.json` and `results/step0_v04/{new_split,
   freesound_doors, tv_speech, tv_speaker_exclude, rir_positions}.json`; the training loaders also read
   `results/step0_v04/excluded_units.json`. The line check of that first set is
   `src/step0_check_batches.py prepare | stage2 | collect` (checker prompts in
   `configs/checker_prompts_v1.md`).
2. **Lines and episode scripts.**
   - `src/step0v4_generate.py <key json> pools` and `convlines`: answer lines, keep-going lines and
     per-conversation lines.
   - `src/step0v4_lines.py prepare`, then the checker's answers (`configs/checker_prompts_v2.md`),
     then `collect` and `assign`: check and split the lines (`results/step0_v04/lines_v04.json`).
   - `src/step0v4_generate.py <key json> episodes` (and `episodes2` for test and replication): the
     multi-turn scripts.
   - `src/step0v4_episodes.py validate`, the `prepare` commands, then `collect`: structure check,
     decision points and the checker's causal check (`results/step0_v04/episode_check.json`).
3. **Rendering.** `src/p1v4_render_split.py train --draw D` for D = 0 to 11, then
   `src/p1v4_render_split.py dev`, `test` and `replication` (library code in `src/p1v4_units.py` and
   `src/p1_render.py`; text-to-speech is cached in `data/p1/tts_cache/`). Each run writes
   `data/p1v4/render/<split>[_dDD]/`. Then, before any training, `src/p1v4_leak_check.py <render
   folders>` (no source crosses splits) and `src/p1v4_shortcut_check.py` (generator settings alone
   cannot predict the answers).
4. **Front-end features** (GPU). `src/p1v4_sed.py train <out dir>` trains the sound-event detector;
   `src/p1v4_feats.py enrol-tts train` and `src/p1v4_feats.py run <folder> <detector>` per render
   folder write the transcripts, speaker embeddings and detected sounds to `data/p1v4/feats/`;
   `src/p1v4_context.py threshold` fixes the speaker threshold on the development set. The JSON files
   in `samples/` include these outputs for their decision points.
